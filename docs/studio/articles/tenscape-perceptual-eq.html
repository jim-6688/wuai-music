十境调音：面向数字音频播放器的听感语义参量均衡系统 TENSCAPE: A Perceptual-Semantic Parametric Equalization System for Digital Audio Players

吾爱高保真实验室　WuAi HiFi Laboratory
白皮书 02 · 音频 DSP · 吾爱高保真实验室 · 2026-11-20
摘要　本文提出并实现了一套面向数字音频播放器的听感语义参量均衡系统——十境调音（WUAI TENSCAPE）。该系统将传统参量均衡器的频率、增益、Q 值三参数，映射为十个以听感描述命名的控制维度（晨昏、深潜、回弹、肌理、唱腔、圆润、顺滑、起落、清亮、天光），使不具备声学专业知识的用户亦能进行风格化调音。系统以 CamillaDSP 的 Biquad 滤波器组为执行内核，采用"语义层／专业层"互斥双模式架构：语义层使用十组固定频点与 Q 值的滤波器，专业层保留完全开放的标准十段均衡器并作为 AutoEQ 测量校正的落点。本文详细阐述十境的维度定义、滑块到滤波器参数的线性映射、倾斜（tilt）滤波器的反向搁架实现，以及圆形旋钮交互设计。经参数级验证，各维度映射精度与互斥性均满足设计预期。 Abstract — This paper presents the design and implementation of a perceptual-semantic parametric equalization system for digital audio players, named TENSCAPE (WUAI TENSCAPE). The system maps the three conventional parameters of a parametric equalizer — frequency, gain, and Q — into ten perceptually named control dimensions, enabling users without acoustic expertise to perform stylistic sound shaping. Built upon a CamillaDSP Biquad filter bank, the system adopts a mutually exclusive dual-mode architecture: a semantic layer using ten fixed-frequency/fixed-Q filters, and a professional layer preserving a fully parametric ten-band EQ as the integration point for AutoEQ measured correction. This paper details the dimension definitions, the linear slider-to-filter mapping, a anti-shelf implementation of the tilt filter, and the circular-knob interaction design. Parameter-level verification confirms mapping accuracy and mode exclusivity.

1 引言1 Introduction

高保真音频回放中，均衡器（EQ）是修正频响与塑造听感的核心工具。传统图形均衡器以固定频点、固定 Q 值的多个频段构成，用户需理解频率与听感之间的对应关系；参量均衡器（PEQ）虽提供频率、增益、Q 值的完全控制，却进一步提高了使用门槛。消费级市场亟需一种既能表达"冷暖、人声靠前、空气感"等主观听感，又能保证工程精度的调音接口。

In high-fidelity audio playback, the equalizer is central to both frequency-response correction and tonal shaping. Graphic equalizers use fixed frequency bands with fixed Q, requiring users to understand the mapping between frequency and perception. Parametric equalizers (PEQ) offer full control over frequency, gain, and Q, but raise the barrier further. The consumer market needs a tuning interface that expresses subjective descriptors such as warmth, vocal forwardness, and airiness while preserving engineering precision.

本文提出的十境调音（TENSCAPE）与海贝音乐 MSEB 属同类听感调音思路——将参数均衡包装为听感语言——但为独立设计与实现，在命名体系、参数可见性与系统架构上采取差异化路线：十位维度采用东方意象命名并附白话副标题；提供"语义／专业"互斥双模式，前者面向普通用户，后者面向测量校正；并叠加交叉馈送、FIR 房间校正等本系统独有能力。

The proposed TENSCAPE shares the same perceptual-tuning approach as HiBy's MSEB — wrapping parametric EQ in perceptual language — but is an independent design and implementation, differentiated in nomenclature, parameter visibility, and architecture: the ten dimensions use Eastern imagery with plain-language subtitles; a mutually exclusive semantic/professional dual-mode serves novices and measurement-based correction respectively; and capabilities unique to this system, such as crossfeed and FIR room correction, are layered on top.

2 技术背景2 Technical Background

2.1 参量均衡与 AutoEQ

参量均衡器由 RBJ Audio EQ Cookbook 定义的 Biquad 滤波器构成，核心参数为：中心频率 f₀（20 Hz–20 kHz）、增益 Gain（典型 ±12 dB）、品质因数 Q（0.2–20）及滤波器类型（Peaking、Low Shelf、High Shelf）。AutoEQ 项目以 MIT 许可发布大量耳机的测量校正数据，其 ParametricEQ.txt 输出即一组任意频率＋Q 的参数滤波器，FixedBandEQ.txt 则为十段固定频点、Q = 1.41 的方案。

A parametric equalizer comprises Biquad filters as defined by the RBJ Audio EQ Cookbook, parameterized by center frequency (20 Hz–20 kHz), gain (typically ±12 dB), quality factor Q (0.2–20), and filter type (peaking, low shelf, high shelf). The MIT-licensed AutoEQ project publishes measured correction data for headphones; its ParametricEQ.txt is a set of arbitrary frequency/Q filters, while FixedBandEQ.txt is a ten-band fixed-frequency scheme with Q = 1.41.

2.2 海贝 MSEB 的实现原理

MSEB（Mage Sound 8-ball Tuning）是海贝自研的多维度调音系统。根据 MSEB 作者（海贝员工）于 2022 年公开的技术说明及第三方实测，其本质是"一组固定频点与 Q 值的 PEQ 滤波器，滑块值映射为增益"：总温度为倾斜（tilt）滤波，低频下潜为 70 Hz 临界阻尼低搁架，空气感为 10 kHz 临界阻尼高搁架，其余为不同 Q 值的点均衡器。各频带刻意设计得足够宽，彼此交融，从而以十个维度覆盖广泛的听感塑形空间。

MSEB (Mage Sound 8-ball Tuning) is HiBy's proprietary multi-dimensional tuning system. According to technical details disclosed in 2022 by its author (a HiBy employee) and third-party measurements, it is essentially "a set of PEQ filters with predetermined frequency and Q, with slider values mapped to gain": overall temperature is a tilt filter, bass extension a critically damped low shelf at 70 Hz, air a critically damped high shelf at 10 kHz, and the remainder point EQs of varying Q. The bands are intentionally wide enough to blend, covering a broad perceptual shaping space with ten dimensions.

3.2 双模式互斥架构

音色层采用互斥双模式，由状态字段 tone_mode 控制：semantic（十境）与 pro（原十段 EQ）二选一。互斥的目的是避免对同一频段重复处理造成频响畸变与相位问题。两种模式的参数均独立保存在持久化状态中，切换时不丢失。房间校正 FIR、去加重、交叉馈送、声道平衡与输出电平位于音色层之外，与任一模式叠加，不受切换影响。

The tone layer adopts a mutually exclusive dual-mode architecture controlled by the state field tone_mode: semantic (TENSCAPE) and pro (original ten-band EQ), with exactly one active. Exclusivity prevents response distortion and phase issues from processing the same bands twice. Parameters of both modes are stored independently and preserved across switches. FIR room correction, de-emphasis, crossfeed, channel balance, and output gain reside outside the tone layer and overlay either mode.

4.2 倾斜（tilt）滤波器的反向搁架实现

晨昏（Shade）为全频倾斜：在 dB–log f 平面上绕中频旋转整条频响曲线。CamillaDSP 无原生 tilt 类型，本系统以一对反向宽搁架实现——低频搁架切点 180 Hz、高频搁架切点 6 kHz，Q 均为 0.707，增益互为相反数：

Shade is a full-band tilt: rotating the entire response curve around a mid frequency in the dB–log f plane. CamillaDSP has no native tilt type; the system implements it with a pair of anti-phase wide shelving filters — low-shelf corner 180 Hz, high-shelf corner 6 kHz, both Q 0.707, with opposite gains:

G_tilt = (s / 100) × 6 dB
LowShelf(180 Hz, gain = −G_tilt)　+　HighShelf(6 kHz, gain = +G_tilt)

s > 0 为冷亮（压低、抬高高频），s < 0 为暖暗（抬高低频、压低高频），与海贝 MSEB 作者所述"toward treble (cool) or bass (warm)"一致。

s > 0 yields a cool/bright tilt (attenuate bass, lift treble); s < 0 a warm/dark tilt, consistent with the MSEB author's description of "toward treble (cool) or bass (warm)".

5 实现5 Implementation

5.1 双模式互斥实现

配置生成器 dsp_config.py 根据 tone_mode 分支构建滤波器：semantic 模式调用 build_ten_scenes() 生成十境组（滑块中位附近时跳过该滤波器，零处理直通）；pro 模式生成原十段 Peaking（32 Hz–16 kHz，Q = 1.41）。服务端 server.py 提供 tone_gain、tone_mode、tone_preset 三类操作，并对滑块值做 −100 至 +100 的钳制。状态结构向后兼容：缺少 tone_mode 的旧配置默认进入语义模式（十境全 0 即直通）。

The configuration generator dsp_config.py branches on tone_mode: semantic calls build_ten_scenes() to generate the TENSCAPE bank (skipping filters near the neutral slider position for zero-processing pass-through); pro generates the ten-band Peaking bank (32 Hz–16 kHz, Q = 1.41). The server exposes tone_gain, tone_mode, and tone_preset operations with slider clamping to [−100, 100]. State is backward compatible: legacy configurations lacking tone_mode default to semantic mode (all-zero sliders mean pass-through).

5.2 圆形旋钮交互

十境与专业段均以圆形旋钮呈现，复用音量旋钮的视觉语言（conic-gradient 渐变环、指示圆点、中央数值）。与单向音量旋钮不同，均衡旋钮为双极性：环覆盖 270°，中位置于正顶部；正值沿顺时针填充右侧，负值逆时针填充左侧。指针角度经 atan2 计算并吸附至底部缺口两端；滚轮、双击归零、键盘方向键均受支持。交互在 Web 前端完成，提交时调用既有 DSP 接口，不改变后端信号处理链路。

Both TENSCAPE and professional bands are rendered as circular knobs, reusing the volume knob's visual language (conic-gradient ring, indicator dot, central value). Unlike the unidirectional volume knob, EQ knobs are bipolar: the ring spans 270° with neutral at top; positive values fill clockwise to the right, negative counterclockwise to the left. Pointer angle is computed via atan2 and snapped at the bottom gap; wheel, double-click-to-zero, and arrow keys are supported. Interaction is implemented in the web front-end and invokes existing DSP endpoints, leaving the back-end signal chain unchanged.

7 结论7 Conclusion

十境调音（WUAI TENSCAPE）以听感语义层降低了数播调音的使用门槛，同时以互斥双模式保留了专业参数控制的精度与 AutoEQ 校正能力。其创新点在于：东方意象命名与白话副标题的语义体系、语义／专业互斥架构、双极性圆形旋钮交互，以及与 FIR 房间校正等独有能力的可叠加性。该系统已在本项目数播端完成工程实现与参数级验证，后续工作包括听感盲听评测与 AutoEQ 参数化模式的进一步整合。

TENSCAPE lowers the barrier to DSP tuning on digital audio players through a perceptual-semantic layer, while retaining professional parameter control and AutoEQ correction via a mutually exclusive dual-mode architecture. Its contributions include an Eastern-imagery nomenclature with plain subtitles, the exclusive dual-mode design, bipolar circular-knob interaction, and composability with unique capabilities such as FIR room correction. The system has been engineered and parameter-verified in this project; future work includes listening-panel evaluation and deeper integration of an AutoEQ parametric mode.
